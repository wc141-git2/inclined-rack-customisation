A Four-tier Rack for Serological Pipettes

In busy research and clinical environments, multi-size serological pipettes are essential everyday consumables, making organized, accessible, and contamination-free storage critical to laboratory efficiency. Rytros addresses this need with a flexible four-tier rack designed to securely store standard serological pipettes from 1 mL to 50 mL. Its vertical, space-saving design maximizes valuable lab real estate, fitting neatly on crowded benchtops or near cabinets while keeping pipettes organized and readily accessible.

Current Product Specifications

  • Overall dimensions: (Width × Depth × Height) approx. 130 × 270 × 370 mm
  • Shelf inner width: Approx. 120 mm
  • Shelf inner length: Approx. 255 mm
  • Layout: Three fixed shelves with an open shelf at the top
  • Space between shelves:
    • Assembly A: Approx. 95 mm, 95 mm, and 115 mm
    • Assembly B: Approx. 95 mm between shelves
